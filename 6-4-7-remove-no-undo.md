# 6.4.7 · Remove (no undo)

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.4 · Add and remove people

**Remove from campaign takes them out on the spot.** There is **no confirmation dialog and no message afterwards**. The only thing that tells you it worked is the count: *Showing 15 of 15* becomes *Showing 12 of 12* and the **People** badge follows. Nothing asks you twiceOne click removes everyone ticked, and there is no undo. **Read the count on the action bar before you click**, especially after using **Select all N matching**, which reaches past the rows you can see.
