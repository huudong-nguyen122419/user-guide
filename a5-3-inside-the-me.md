# A5.3 · Inside the ME

> A5 · Manage a marketing email (Admin)

## A5.3.1 · Open it

Header: editable **name**, **timezone**, **description** and **owner** with the **⚙** badge and the **Active** switch. Tabs: **Inbox, Template, People, Preview, Email Queues and Timeline**. **Two are conditional**: **Preview** and **Email Queues** do not always show. A fresh Draft can legitimately show neither, so do not read their absence as a fault; the exact trigger is still being pinned down.

![ME detail header](admm-03-detail.png)

*A5.3.1: ① owner + ⚙ + the Active switch · ② the tab row · ③ Force Send and the step's delete icon.*

## A5.3.2 · Template

holds the single step. It carries the **Force Send** button from [A5.7.3 ↗](a5-7-run-force-send.md) plus a **🗑 delete** on the step, deleting it empties the ME, so only do that on a Draft.

## A5.3.3 · ⚙ on the owner avatar → change the sending account

Same control as a campaign: a list of Fintalent accounts, the current one excluded; picking one changes the account the blast sends **From**.

![Sending account picker](admm-04-sender.png)

*A5.3.3: ① the ⚙ badge · ② the account list. Set this before Force Send: it decides the From address and where replies land.*
