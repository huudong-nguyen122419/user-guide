# 6.5.4 · Mark done ✓

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.5 · Preview & review

**Mark the contact done.** When you have read their steps and written anything you wanted to write, click the **green ✓** on their row. It asks *Done this contact?*, and the row then carries a **(Done)** tag with the tick replaced by an **↺** undo icon. Clicking the undo asks *Undone this contact?* and clears it. **Done is a note to yourself.** It does not send anything, hold anything back, or change what the contact receives. It is how you keep track of who you have already been through, which matters on a list you cannot finish in one sitting. The **Done** filter at the top then splits the list into reviewed and not.
