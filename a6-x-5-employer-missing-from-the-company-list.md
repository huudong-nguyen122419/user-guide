# A6.x.5 · Employer missing from the company list

> A6 · Approve a new talent (decides Active or Passive) → A6.x · Edge cases

**Most current employers are not linked, so company flags are missing.** Roughly **63%** of talents have a current role whose employer was never matched to the Mergermarket company list — no Sponsor / Portfolio / Corporate flag to read. For those people the title and the free-text company description are the only evidence. This is also why the Work Experience filter cannot replace the title check: every one of the strongest buy-side hits found on production sat at an **unflagged** company.
