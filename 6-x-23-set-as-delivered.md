# 6.x.23 · Set as Delivered

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.x · Edge cases

**Send one queued email now: Set as Delivered.** Queued mail waits for the start of the day in the campaign's timezone. To push a single one out without waiting, open the **⋮** menu on its row and choose **Set as Delivered**. It leaves **In Queue** and lands under **Delivered**. Useful when somebody is waiting on a specific email, or when a demo cannot wait for tomorrow morning. **It affects one row.** To move a whole step, that is **Force Send** on the Steps tab ([6.7 ↗](6-7-email-queue.md)).

> **The name says less than the action does**
>
> *Set as Delivered* reads like a bookkeeping change, a status you are correcting by hand. **It sends the email.** Treat it as a send button that has been labelled as a status.
