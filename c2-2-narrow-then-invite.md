# C2.2 · Narrow, then invite

> C2 · Source and invite talents

The pre-set filters get you to the right population, not to the right people. Add what the brief actually needs on top: **Functional Expertise**, **Industry & Sector**, **Company Background**, **Years of Experience**, **Countries**, **Cities**, **Timezone Offsets**, **Languages**, **Has Contract**.

**Timezone matters more than it looks** on a project that says it wants overlap with the client's working day. It is one of the few filters that rules people out for a reason nobody can fix.

Invited people then show up on the project's **Invited Talents** tab, and anyone who accepts turns into an application ([C3 ↗](c3-work-the-applications.md)).

Inviting talents one project at a time from the talent side is [B1 ↗](b1-invite-talents-to-a-project.md). Same destination, different door.
