# 4b.x.2 · Two clocks on every row

> 4b · Schedule a call → 4b.x · Edge cases

**Every row shows the time twice, and the second one is the safety net.** The first line is the start in the meeting's timezone (e.g. *31 Jul 2026, 15:00:00 GMT+7*); underneath, in orange, the same moment in **GMT+0** (*Fri, 31 Jul 2026 08:00:00 GMT+0*). Since the create form defaults to **your** timezone rather than the contact's, the GMT+0 line is how you check you booked the hour you actually agreed.
