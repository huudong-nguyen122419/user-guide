# B3.x.6 · Open invoice from Timesheets

> B3 · Timesheet → invoice (Admin) → B3.x · Edge cases

**Open the invoice straight from the Timesheets page, no detour through the menu.** After creation each talent row carries the invoice block (number, timesheet range, hours, amount and status). **Click that block** → the **Update Client Invoice** drawer opens in place, already offering: Faster than the **Client Invoices → search** route in [B3.4 ↗](b3-4-review-invoice-and-pdf.md) when you have just created the invoice and want to check it immediately.

- the **⤓ download PDF** button next to the title;

- the **status** dropdown (Draft → Issued → Paid…) and the **Update** button;

- the **PROJECT / CLIENT / TALENT / CONTRACT / TIMESHEET** summary bar and the links **View Timesheets, View Contract, View Project, View Talent and View Client**;

- the whole invoice form, editable.
