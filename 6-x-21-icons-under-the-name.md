# 6.x.21 · Icons under the name

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.x · Edge cases

**The icons under the contact’s name.** The little row of icons in the Preview header opens this person somewhere else, without losing your place in the campaign. Same row of icons, same behaviour, in the Marketing Email flow at [7.x.4 ↗](7-x-4-quick-jump-icons.md).

| Icon | Where it takes you |
|---|---|
| **View Inbox** | the **Conversations** tab on their contact record: every email and call thread with them. |
| **View Contact Info** | the contact drawer: phone, location, LinkedIn, experience, work history. |
| **View Company** | the company drawer: industry, website, classifications, email domains. |
| **View on LinkedIn** | their LinkedIn profile, in a new tab. |

> **A missing icon is information**
>
> You will not always see four. An icon is **only there when the contact has that piece of data**, so no company icon means no company is linked, and no LinkedIn icon means no LinkedIn URL on the record. **Count them before you conclude the record is complete.**
