# A6.x.9 · What Passive does not affect

> A6 · Approve a new talent (decides Active or Passive) → A6.x · Edge cases

**Going Passive does not stop any work already under way.** Passive only removes someone from the project-invitation flow. Running contracts, timesheets and invoices are unaffected, see [B3 ↗](b3-timesheet-invoice-admin.md).
