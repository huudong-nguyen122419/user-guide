# 6.8.7 · Bounced / dropped

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.8 · Verify: Inbox & replies

**Bounced / Dropped: the ones that never arrived.** These are not replies. Nobody read anything. The send failed, and the usual reasons are a wrong or dead address, a full or closed mailbox, a receiving server that refused it, or something going wrong mid-send. Click **Bounced / Dropped** on the rail and the list is exactly the failed sends. Read the **Bounce / Dropped Rate** on the campaign row the same way: it is a count of people to chase, not a score to feel bad about.

> **Sending them again is not built yet**
>
> There is no retry on this screen. Until there is: get the **address corrected** on the contact, flag it to Admin / Ops if you cannot edit it yourself, and reach the person another way, a [1:1 email (Flow 3) ↗](3-send-a-1-1-email.md) to an address that works, or [WhatsApp / LinkedIn (3.x.4) ↗](3-x-4-whatsapp-linkedin-instead.md). Once the address is right, Admin / Ops can put them into the next run.
