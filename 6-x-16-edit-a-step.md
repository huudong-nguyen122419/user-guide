# 6.x.16 · Edit a step

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.x · Edge cases

**Edit a step you already created.** On the **Steps** tab, click the **variant row** under the step (the row marked **A**). The same editor opens with your subject and body loaded, and two things change: the button now reads **Save** instead of **Create**, and a **Force Update** control appears next to it. **Force Update decides whether the edit reaches previews that already exist.** Leave it alone and your change applies from here on; tick it and it overwrites the per-contact copies already generated, **including personalization someone typed by hand**. Full warning at [6.x.12 ↗](6-x-12-force-update.md).
