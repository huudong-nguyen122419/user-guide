# 6.4.5 · Add to campaign

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.4 · Add and remove people

**Click Add to campaign.** They appear on the **People** tab and the campaign's **People** count on the list goes up to match.

> **The button stays greyed out until both halves are answered**
>
> You need **a campaign ticked on the left** *and* **at least one person ticked on the right**. Miss either and the button simply does not respond, with no message telling you which half is missing. If it looks dead, check the left panel first: that is the half people forget.
