# 7.7a · Find bounces & drops

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email → 7.7 · Verify in Conversations

A **bounce / drop** means that contact **never got the email**. Check for them after every send — step by step:

## 7.7a.1 · Spot it on the list

On **Marketing Emails**, read the **Bounce/Dropped Rate** column — anything **above 0%** (count in brackets) means failed sends inside.

![Bounce/Dropped Rate column on the ME list](sdrx-me-26-bounce-column.png)

*7.7a.1 — the Bounce/Dropped Rate column on the ME list.*

## 7.7a.2 · See exactly who

Open the ME → **Inbox** tab → click the **Bounced / Dropped** category (left rail). The filter applies and **everyone listed is a failed send**.

![ME Inbox — Bounced / Dropped category](sdrx-me-25-bounce-inbox.png)

*7.7a.2 — ① Inbox tab → ② Bounced / Dropped → ③ the filter applies → ④ everyone here failed.*

## 7.7a.3 · Fix & re-reach

For each failed contact: get the **email address corrected** on their profile (flag it to Admin / Ops if you can't edit it) — then **reach them another way**: a [1:1 email (Flow 3) ↗](3-send-a-1-1-email.md) to a working address, or [WhatsApp / LinkedIn (3.x.4) ↗](3-x-4-whatsapp-linkedin-instead.md). Once fixed, Admin / Ops can include them in the next send.
