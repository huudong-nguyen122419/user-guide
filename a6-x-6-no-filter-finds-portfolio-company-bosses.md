# A6.x.6 · No filter finds portfolio-company bosses

> A6 · Approve a new talent (decides Active or Passive) → A6.x · Edge cases

**No filter finds bosses of companies a fund owns.** These are the people in **group C**, the CEO or CFO of a business a PE fund has bought. The box that ought to find them, *Corporate / Industry → PE-Backed / Portfolio Company*, is **attached to nobody at all**: zero people, everywhere. Reach them a different way: the **Work Experience** filter with *company type = Portfolio* and *Currently*, which returns 156 people.
