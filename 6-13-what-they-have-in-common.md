# 6‡.13 · What they have in common

> 6‡ · Manage a campaign, issues

Read together rather than one at a time, they fall into three groups, and the groups matter more than the individual tickets.

| Pattern | Items | The shape of it |
|---|---|---|
| **A filter that answers instead of failing** | CMP-13, CMP-14, CMP-15, CMP-19 | Four of the twelve. Every one returns a clean, confident, wrong answer: **0 of 0**, **No items found**, or the full list unchanged. Nothing on screen distinguishes *nothing matched* from *the query could not run*, so the reader believes the number. |
| **Two places that count the same thing differently** | CMP-11, CMP-16 | Replies against people, sidesteps against steps. In both the raw count is right and the derived figure is wrong, which is the hardest kind to notice. |
| **The screen knows something it does not say** | CMP-12, CMP-18, CMP-20, CMP-09 | The lifecycle exists but is not on the row. The step exists but is not drawn. The menu item exists but does nothing. The folder exists and should not be visible at all. |

The one to fix first is **CMP-18**: it is the only item that stops the flow rather than misinforming it. After that the filter group, because four broken filters teach an SDR to stop trusting filters.
