# B3.x.5 · No submit, no billing

> B3 · Timesheet → invoice (Admin) → B3.x · Edge cases

**Nothing can be billed until the talent submits.** Hours that are logged but not submitted sit in **pending submission** on the talent side — they never reach **Unbilled**, so they do not appear when you create an invoice. Ask the talent to submit first.
