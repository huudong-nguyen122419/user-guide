# 6.x.24 · Exclude a category

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.x · Edge cases

**Hide a category instead of showing it.** On the Inbox rail, one click on a category **includes** it: green **✓**, and the list shows only that group. **Click the same category again** and it flips to **exclude**: the list now shows everyone *except* that group. Useful for reading what is left rather than what is done. Exclude **Not Interested**, **Opted Out** and **Do Not Contact** and what remains is the part of the campaign still worth working. It is the same include / exclude behaviour as the **Lifecycles** filter itself ([8.1 ↗](8-1-list-members-bar.md)), which is what the rail is driving. A third click clears it. If a count on the rail stops matching what you see in the list, check whether a category is sitting on exclude, then use **Clear all**.
