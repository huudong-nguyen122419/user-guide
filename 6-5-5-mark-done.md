# 6.5.5 · Mark done ✓

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.5 · Preview & review

**Mark the contacts done.** Once you have been through them, tick **All** at the top of the contact list. A **Bulk Actions** button appears beside the count. Open it and choose **Mark all as Done**. **Every row then carries a (Done) tag**, and the green tick on the row is replaced by an **↺** undo arrow. That is the whole signal: the **Done** filter at the top now splits the list into reviewed and not. **What Done means:** these contacts have been read and their emails are ready to go out. It is a record of your review, not an instruction to the campaign. Marking someone Done does not send anything, hold anything back, or change a single word they receive. What it buys you is knowing where you stopped on a list you cannot get through in one sitting. **One at a time works too.** The green **✓** on a single row asks *Done this contact?* and tags just that person; the undo arrow asks *Undone this contact?*. Worth it for one or two, not for a list.

| Menu item | When you reach for it |
|---|---|
| **Mark all as Done** | you have read everyone’s steps and they are ready to go out. This is the one you use at the end of a review pass. |
| **Mark all as Undone** | something changed after you reviewed, so the review no longer counts. Puts them back to unreviewed and clears the tags. |
| **Mark all as Unpersonalized** | you rewrote the step, so the personal lines were written against an email that no longer exists. Drops the per-contact copy and puts everyone back on the standard step ([6.x.18 ↗](6-x-18-mark-all-as-unpersonalized.md)). |
| **Bulk update steps** | the email needs a change and you do not want to open two hundred contacts to make it. Edit it once, push it to all of them ([6.x.19 ↗](6-x-19-bulk-update-steps.md)). |
| **Remove all from campaign** | they should not be in this campaign at all. They stop receiving anything, and each of them is free to join another campaign. |

> **None of the five asks you to confirm**
>
> The single-row tick pops a dialog. **The bulk versions do not.** Click the menu item and it has already happened to everyone selected, including **Remove all from campaign**, which sits one row below **Bulk update steps** in the same menu. **Read the count on the left before you open the menu.** Your selection is cleared after each action, so a second bulk action means ticking **All** again. Slightly annoying, and the only thing standing between two clicks and the wrong list.
