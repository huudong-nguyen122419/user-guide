# B3.x.2 · "Total Hours Worked"

> B3 · Timesheet → invoice (Admin) → B3.x · Edge cases

**What “Total Hours Worked” means on the list page.** The figure in the header and in the table column of the Timesheets page is the **total hours to bill** (the billable number). To see the **actual logged hours**, open **View Timesheets** — only there are *to bill* and *worked* shown as separate lines.
