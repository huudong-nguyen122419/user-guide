# B2.x.5 · Several projects

> B2 · Talent · log & submit timesheet → B2.x · Edge cases

**A talent with several Active contracts at once.** The project bar at the top becomes a **selector**, click it to switch. Things to remember:

- The calendar, the three totals (*submitted / Worked / pending*), the submission queue and the expense reports are all **per project**, switching project replaces everything on screen.

- **Submissions never merge across projects**: each one submits separately, and Admin invoices per contract.

- The URL carries `?projectId=…`: if the numbers look “wrong”, first check the project name in the top bar.
