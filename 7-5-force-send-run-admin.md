# 7.5 · Force Send + Run (Admin)

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email

> **Not your button, and not your call to make early**
>
> Running a ME is an **Admin/Ops** action. Your part is to **ask for it**, and to ask only once [7.4 ↗](7-4-preview-and-mark-reviewed.md) is genuinely finished, every contact previewed, personalised where it matters, and **marked reviewed**. Force Send only reaches reviewed contacts ([A5.x.2 ↗](a5-x-2-force-send-scope.md)), so asking early just sends a smaller batch than you meant. What you hand over: the ME name, that review is complete, and the number you expect to go out. What comes back: the ME switched to **Active** and the queue filled. Everything in [7.6 ↗](7-6-email-queue-3-tabs.md) only exists after this step has happened.

> **No Force Send on the SDR view**
>
> **Force Send is an Admin / Ops action**, on the SDR login there is **no Force Send button** (the ME's ⋮ menu only offers **Edit / View Details**). Your Admin both flips the ME **Draft → Active** and clicks **Force Send**. The Admin side is [Flow A5.8 ↗](a5-8-run-force-send.md).

**Your review is what your Admin is waiting on.** The confirm they get when they flip the ME on counts recipients, email steps and **reviewed**, and warns them that anyone unreviewed “will receive the default template as-is”. At **0 reviewed** the queue is not built at all, silently. Their side, dialog by dialog, is [A5.8.3 ↗](a5-8-run-force-send.md). Once your Admin has force-sent, your job is to **check the queue**:

## 7.5.1

Open the **marketing email detail**.

## 7.5.2

Go to the **Email Queue** tab.

## 7.5.3 · Check the count

the number of queued emails should match the **number of contacts you marked as reviewed** (7.4.5). If it doesn't, something's off; flag it, don't just carry on.
