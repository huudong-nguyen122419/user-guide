# 7.3.3 · Tick → Add to ME

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email → 7.3 · Add people to the ME

**Tick the contacts you want.** A selection bar appears with the count, **Select all N matching** for the whole filtered set, and **Clear**. Of the actions on the right, the one you want is **Add to marketing email** — the neighbours add to a list or a campaign instead, and **Delete contacts** sits two buttons away.
