# A6.x.8 · Wrong company flags

> A6 · Talent Active / Passive → A6.x · Edge cases

**Company type flags come from an external source and can be wrong.** Production has investment banks flagged as *sponsor*, and at least one talent linked to an entirely different company than the one named in their title. When a familiar name produces a surprising result, check the company record before concluding the talent is misclassified.
