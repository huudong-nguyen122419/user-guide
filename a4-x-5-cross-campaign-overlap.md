# A4.x.5 · Cross-campaign overlap

> A4 · Manage a campaign (Admin) → A4.x · Edge cases

**“Cross-campaign” is the number to fear.** A contact sitting in two live campaigns gets two cold emails in the same week. The **Recently contacted and 7 days** line on the campaign catches it. Resolve by pulling the person out of one campaign, not by ignoring the badge.
