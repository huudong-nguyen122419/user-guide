# 6.5 · Preview & review

> 6 · Manage a campaign

Before the campaign runs, **review what every contact will actually get** on the **Preview** tab, the campaign's review gate, exactly like a Marketing Email's.

> **Everything here has a keyboard shortcut**
>
> Reviewing a long list is faster from the keyboard than from the mouse. **Click the keyboard icon** at the top of the contact list, beside the sort controls, and the full list of shortcuts opens. It covers moving between contacts, expanding a step, saving, and the three row actions from [6.x.20 ↗](6-x-20-buttons-on-a-contact-row.md). **Some of them are a single letter and they act at once**, including the one that removes a contact from the campaign, so read the list before you start typing in this tab.
