# 6.9 · Bounces · hot replies

> 6 · Manage a campaign

* **Replies** — read & answer them via [Flow 4 · View & answer replies ↗](4-view-replies-and-answer-replies.md); set the lifecycle so the campaign stops chasing people who said no.

* **Bounce / Dropped?** that address failed — find who and fix it step by step in [6.8a ↗](6-8a-find-bounces-and-drops.md), then reach the person another way with a [1:1 email (Flow 3) ↗](3-send-a-1-1-email.md).

* **Hot reply?** book the meeting — the reply is the point of the whole sequence.
