# 4.x.1 · Reply all

> 4 · View replies & answer replies → 4.x · Edge cases

**Reply all** — the **double ↩↩ arrow** (left of Reply, circled below) answers the sender **plus everyone else on the thread's To/Cc**. Use it only when several people are genuinely in the conversation (an assistant or colleague was copied). Same compose window as Reply, but the **To** line is pre-filled with **every email address in the thread** — so your one reply goes out **from your mailbox to all of them at once**. Hover the button to preview the full recipient list (e.g. `To: oemer.gueven@fintalent.com, dz@dzadvisory.de`), and remove anyone you don't want before you send. Like Reply, the **subject auto-fills** as **RE: [original subject]**.
