# C4.1 · Live work

> C4 · Keep the pipeline moving

Everything approved and running. The columns are the health check, and you can read them without opening anything:

| Column | What a blank means |
|---|---|
| **Interviews** | nobody has met the client yet. Early, or stuck. |
| **Contracts** | nobody has been hired yet. |
| **Client booked a call** | the client has not engaged since it went live. |
| **Channels** / **Emails** | no conversation is running on it at all. |

**A project that is Open with all of those empty is the one to look at first.** It is live, so the client believes something is happening, and nothing is.
