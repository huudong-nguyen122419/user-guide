# C3.2 · Fit score and evidence

> C3 · Work the applications

Each application carries a score out of 10 and a band: **Strong Fit**, **Good Fit**, **Partial Fit** or **Weak Fit**. The chips above the table filter by band, so you can open a project and see only the strong ones.

**The number on its own is not the point. Show evidence is.** Open it and the score breaks into the criteria it was built from, each with its own mark and a weight that says how much it counted, for example **35%**, **25%**, **20%**, **20%**. Those criteria come from the project's **Pillars** ([C1.2 ↗](c1-2-read-the-brief.md)).

> **A Partial Fit can be the right person**
>
> The bands average across every pillar, so somebody who is outstanding on the pillar that actually matters and weak on a pillar nobody cares about lands mid-table. **Read the breakdown before you trust the band**, above all on a project where one requirement is the whole job.

Beside the score sits what you need to judge it: the talent's **requested gross rate**, their status, location, headline, and their expertise counts.
