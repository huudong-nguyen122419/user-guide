# A6.x.4 · Title vs work experience

> A6 · Talent Active / Passive → A6.x · Edge cases

**The title and the work experience disagree.** Common and important. Seen in production: a title reading *"Freelance, former Private Equity Professional"* on a profile whose work experience still shows an **ongoing** role at a PE fund. The title claims they left; the data says they did not. Open the row and check the end date before deciding.
