# 6.10.1 · Who finishes it

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.10 · Finish a campaign

**Finishing is an Admin action.** Two things bring a campaign to it: it has **run to the end of its sequence**, or somebody has **asked for it to stop**. Either way an SDR does not close a campaign, they hand it back the way they handed it over to be run ([6.6 ↗](6-6-hand-it-over-to-be-run.md)). Only a campaign that is **Active** or **Paused** can be finished. A Draft has never run, so there is nothing to close.

> **What an SDR sees instead**
>
> Signed in as an SDR the button is **not there at all**, and the status switch beside it is drawn but **disabled**, so it reads the status back to you without letting you change it. Nothing on screen explains why, so the honest reading of a greyed switch is *this campaign is not mine to start or stop*, which is correct. **The avatar in the campaign header is the owner, not you.** It carries the owner's name and address whoever is looking, which is worth knowing before you conclude from a screenshot that an SDR can press something.
