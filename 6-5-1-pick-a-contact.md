# 6.5.1 · Pick a contact

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.5 · Preview & review

**Open the Preview tab → click a contact** on the left. The right pane loads that contact's header (name, email and title & company and quick-jump icons) with **every step of the sequence** below it as collapsible rows, **Step 1 - Day 1**, **Step 2 - Day 2**, … **Branches show up here too**, under their own heading. A campaign with a sidestep reads **Main Step** first and then **Sidestep: ‹condition›**, so what you are looking at is everything that could reach this person, not only what they are certain to get ([6.x.2 ↗](6-x-2-sidestep-conditional.md)).
