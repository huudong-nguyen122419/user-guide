# B3.x.9 · Talent sees different words

> B3 · Timesheet → invoice (Admin) → B3.x · Edge cases

**The talent sees different words for the same three states, and one of them is misleading.** **Approved reads as “Submitted” on the talent side**, which is the same word they saw before you touched it. So a talent has no way to tell from the label whether you have approved them yet. If they ask, check this screen: do not take their word for the status.

| Admin sees | Talent sees |
|---|---|
| Pending Approval | Waiting Approval |
| **Approved** | **Submitted** |
| Rejected | Rejected |
