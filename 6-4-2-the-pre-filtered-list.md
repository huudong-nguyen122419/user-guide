# 6.4.2 · The pre-filtered list

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.4 · Add and remove people

**You get taken to the contact list, already filtered.** This is the part people do not expect: the button does not open a picker, it navigates you to **Contacts** and switches on a filter chip for you, **Campaign : No**. So what you are looking at is **only people who are not in any campaign yet**. **Why it does that:** a contact can sit in **one campaign at a time**. Filtering them out up front means you cannot pick someone who is already committed elsewhere. Any filter chips left over from your last visit to Contacts are still there. Read the chip row before you trust the count.
