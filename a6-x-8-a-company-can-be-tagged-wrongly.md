# A6.x.8 · A company can be tagged wrongly

> A6 · Approve a new talent (decides Active or Passive) → A6.x · Edge cases

**A company can be tagged with the wrong type.** The tags come from an outside data source, so they are not always right. The company data has investment banks flagged as *sponsor*, and at least one talent linked to an entirely different company than the one named in their title. When a familiar name produces a surprising result, check the company record before concluding the talent is misclassified.
