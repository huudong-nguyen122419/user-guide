# 6.2.5 · Add follow-ups

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.2 · Build the sequence

Add follow-ups with **+ Create new step**. A follow-up usually waits a day or two — e.g. **Step 2 - Day 2 · Wait for 1 day**. Build the whole sequence this way.
