# A6.x.4 · Title says one thing, jobs another

> A6 · Approve a new talent (decides Active or Passive) → A6.x · Edge cases

**The headline says one thing, the job history says another.** Common and important. Seen in practice: a title reading *"Freelance, former Private Equity Professional"* on a profile whose work experience still shows an **ongoing** role at a PE fund. The title claims they left; the data says they did not. Open the row and check the end date before deciding.
