# 3.1.3 · Conversations → New Email

> 3 · Send a 1:1 email → 3.1 · Four ways into compose

3.1.3Way 3: From the Conversations rail (see history first)

- **Open the contact** → on the right edge click the vertical **CONVERSATIONS** tab to expand the rail.

- In the rail click **New Email** → compose opens, with the existing thread history right there. **Way 3**: the Conversations rail: thread list + **New Email** (and ↩ reply arrows).
