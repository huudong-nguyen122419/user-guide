# 4b.x.1 · No reschedule

> 4b · Schedule a call → 4b.x · Edge cases

**There is no reschedule.** The row menu offers **Cancel call** and nothing else — no edit, no move, no change of duration. A new time means **cancel the old booking and create a new one**, which produces a **new Zoom link**. Send the new link; the old one is dead. Say so in the email, or they will join an empty room from their calendar entry.
