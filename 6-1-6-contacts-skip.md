# 6.1.6 · Contacts → Skip

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.1 · Create (folder first)

**Contacts → Skip.** Wizard step **1 Contacts** is where recipients would be loaded. On the **SDR login it's empty** — click **Skip** to go straight to **Steps**; you add people later ([6.4](6-4-add-people.md)) or Admin/Ops loads them. (Same limitation as Marketing Emails.)
