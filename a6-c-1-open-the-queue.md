# A6-C.1 · Open the queue

> A6 · Approve a new talent (decides Active or Passive) → A6-C · Review the In Review queue

**Open the queue.** **User Management → Talents**, then the **N In Review** button at the top right. It is the only way in; there is no In Review tab on the list. Clicking it opens a drawer showing **one profile at a time** rather than filtering the table. For scale: **280 waiting** against **9,036 talents** on the day this was walked. The queue is small next to the base, which is why it is worth clearing properly rather than quickly.
