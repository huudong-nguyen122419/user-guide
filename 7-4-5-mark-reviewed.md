# 7.4.5 · Mark reviewed

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email → 7.4 · Preview & mark reviewed

When the batch is done: **select the contacts** (**All** at the top selects everyone) → **Bulk Actions** → **Mark as reviewed**. That sets **Content Reviewed** on them.
