# C4.4 · Finished work

> C4 · Keep the pipeline moving

Move a project to **Closed** when the work is done, and use **Archived** for everything that ended without a hire. The two say different things later: **Closed** is a project that worked, and the tab is small enough to read at any time.

**Closing is a decision somebody has to make.** Nothing closes a project on its own, so the Open tab grows until a person tidies it, and every stale row makes the tab less useful for [C4.1](c4-1-live-work.md).
