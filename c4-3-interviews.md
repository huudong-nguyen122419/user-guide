# C4.3 · Interviews

> C4 · Keep the pipeline moving

**Project Management → Interviews** lists every meeting between a client and a talent, with **All**, **Upcoming**, **Past**, **Cancelled** and **Concluded**.

> **Cancelled is worth counting**
>
> In one snapshot, **100 of 253 interviews were Cancelled**, against 101 Concluded and 52 Past. **Roughly two in five never happened.** A cancelled interview is a project that lost a week, and it does not show up anywhere on the Projects list.

Read the tab as a queue rather than a report: an **Upcoming** count of zero on a project that is supposed to be interviewing means nothing is booked.
