# 6.x.17 · Move to other campaign

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.x · Edge cases

**Move to other campaign.** The same action bar on the **People** tab carries **Move to other campaign** next to Remove. It hands the people you ticked to a different campaign in one action, instead of removing them here and adding them there. **Noted, not yet written up.** The steps and what it does to their place in the sequence have not been walked through, so treat this as a pointer rather than an instruction.
