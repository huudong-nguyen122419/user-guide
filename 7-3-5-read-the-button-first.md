# 7.3.5 · Read the button first

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email → 7.3 · Add people to the ME

**⚠ Read the button before you click it — it will not add everyone you ticked.** Confirm reads **Add 2 of 6 contacts**, and directly above it: **Not ticked by default: 4 Missing + Invalid**. Only the **Valid** bucket is pre-ticked. Contacts with a missing or unusable address are carried into the drawer but left **unticked on purpose**, so selecting six people and adding two is the expected outcome, not a fault. Measured on UAT: six selected → `Valid 2 · Missing + Invalid 4 · Platform Signup 0` → button `Add 2 of 6 contacts`. **If you want the excluded ones anyway**, open the **Missing + Invalid** tab and tick them yourself — but fix the address first, or they will bounce ([7.7a ↗](7-7-verify-in-conversations.md)).
