# 7.8 · Replies · bounce · drop

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email

1. **7.8.1** — **Watch the Inbox.** Replies to this ME land in its own **Inbox** tab as well as your main one. Read and answer them the usual way — [Flow 4 ↗](4-view-replies-and-answer-replies.md).

2. **7.8.2** — **Treat any Bounce / Dropped as a to-do, not a statistic.** That send failed, so the person never saw it. Find out who in [7.7a ↗](7-7-verify-in-conversations.md), then reach them another way: a 1:1 email ([Flow 3 ↗](3-send-a-1-1-email.md)) or WhatsApp / LinkedIn ([3.x.4 ↗](3-x-4-whatsapp-linkedin-instead.md)).

3. **7.8.3** — **A rate of 0% is not the same as a blank one.** Blank means nothing has been sent yet; **0%** means mail went out and nobody opened or replied — that is a content problem, and it is worth saying so before the next send.
