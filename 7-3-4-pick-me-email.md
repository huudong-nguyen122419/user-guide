# 7.3.4 · Pick ME + email

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email → 7.3 · Add people to the ME

In the drawer: **choose the ME**, then **choose each contact and which email address to use**. A contact can have several addresses — a ME sends to **one address per contact**, so pick deliberately. The addresses are grouped in three tabs:

- **Valid** — verified addresses; use these.

- **Missing + Invalid** — no address, or one that failed validation.

- **Platform sign up** — the address they signed up to the platform with.
