# 6.x.18 · Mark all as Unpersonalized

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.x · Edge cases

**Mark all as Unpersonalized.** In **Bulk Actions** ([6.5.4 ↗](6-5-4-mark-done.md)), it strips the per-contact copy off everyone selected and puts them back on the standard step. Useful after a rewrite, when the personal lines were written against the old email and no longer make sense. **It runs without asking, and there is no undo.** Anything anyone typed for a specific contact is gone. If only some of them are stale, fix those by hand instead.
