# 4b.x.2 · No Zoom on your account

> 4b · Schedule a call → 4b.x · Edge cases

**Nothing happens when you try to create a meeting — your account has no Zoom.** The meeting is created on a **Zoom account tied to you as host**. An SDR whose admin record carries no Zoom user id has nothing to create the meeting on, so the save fails. **You cannot fix this yourself.** Ask an Admin to connect Zoom for your account; there is no setting on your side. Until it is done, either have somebody else host the call, or send a link from your own Zoom by hand — and note that a booking made outside Fintalent will not appear on the Meetings page or on the contact. Reported behaviour on internal Fintalent SDR accounts; the working account used for this guide has Zoom connected, so the exact wording of the failure is not captured here.
