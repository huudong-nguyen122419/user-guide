# 6.8.6 · Chips that end it

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.8 · Verify: Inbox & replies

**Chips that end the conversation: Not Interested, Opted Out, Do Not Contact.** A campaign is not one email. **Later steps keep going to anyone still on it**, so a person who has said no and stayed on the campaign gets chased again by the next step. Take them off. Removing them from the campaign does not remove the label. The lifecycle stays on the contact, which is what stops the next campaign from picking them up.

> **There is no action for this on the Inbox yet**
>
> You can see the group here, but you cannot act on it here. Until that is built, remove them from the **Preview** tab, where the **Lifecycles** filter and **Bulk Actions → Remove all from campaign** both exist ([6.5 ↗](6-5-preview-and-review.md)), or one at a time from **People** ([6.4b ↗](6-4b-remove-people.md)).
