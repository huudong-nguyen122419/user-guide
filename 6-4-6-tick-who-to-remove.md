# 6.4.6 · Tick who to remove

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.4b · Remove people

**Go to the People tab and tick the people you want out.** As soon as anything is ticked, an action bar appears above the table. It shows what you have selected on the left, and what you can do with them on the right. The one you want is **Remove from campaign**.

![People tab with Remove from campaign on the action bar](sdrx-camp-43-people-actions.png)

*6.4.6: tick the rows on the People tab, then Remove from campaign.*
