# 6.6 · Hand it over to be run

> 6 · Manage a campaign

**You do not run the campaign. Admin / Ops does.** Your part finishes the moment the review is finished, and the handover is four steps:

## 6.6.1 · Finish the review

Every contact you intend to mail has been read ([6.5 ↗](6-5-preview-and-review.md)), anybody who should sit out a step has been skipped ([6.5.3 ↗](6-5-3-skip-a-step.md)).

## 6.6.2 · Mark those contacts done

This is the signal, not a formality. **Done is how Admin knows which contacts you have actually looked at**, and the run dialog counts the ones you have not ([6.5.5 ↗](6-5-5-mark-done.md)).

## 6.6.3 · Tell Admin / Ops it is ready

Nothing notifies them on its own. A campaign sitting in **Draft** with everything marked done looks exactly like one nobody has touched.

## 6.6.4 · They review what they want to review, then run it

The campaign moves from **Draft** to **Active** and the first step starts going out. What happens on their side: [A4.6 ↗](a4-6-run-a-campaign.md).

> **Anything left unmarked becomes a question**
>
> Admin sees *N contact are not marked as done* on the run dialog and has to decide whether to send anyway or come back to you. **Leave a contact unmarked only when you mean to**, and say why when you hand over.

After it runs, your job becomes watching: the queue ([6.7 ↗](6-7-email-queue.md)) and the replies ([6.8 ↗](6-8-verify-inbox-and-replies.md)).
