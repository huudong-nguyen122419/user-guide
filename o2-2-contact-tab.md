# O2.2 · Contact tab

> O2 · Create a list

## O2.2

At the top, click the **Contact** tab. **Why this tab:** the page holds **three kinds of list** — **Talent** (candidates), **Company** (companies), and **Contact** (people you email). When you click **+ Create New** next, the modal's **Type** field is **locked to whichever tab you're on** — you can't change it there. An outreach list must be a **Contact** list, so stand on the **Contact** tab first; otherwise you'd create a Talent/Company list you can't use for outreach.
