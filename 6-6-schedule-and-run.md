# 6.6 · Schedule & run

> 6 · Manage a campaign

Running is Admin / OpsAs the SDR you can move the campaign **Draft → Scheduled** (the status dropdown, top-right). Actually **turning it on**: the **Enabled** toggle on the list, and sending is an **Admin / Ops** action (the Admin side is [Flow A4.6 ↗](a4-6-run-the-campaign-enabled.md)), like force-sending a Marketing Email. Your job after that is to **monitor**.

![Draft to Scheduled status](sdrx-camp-11-status.png)

*6.6: the status control: Draft → Scheduled. Enabling / running is the Admin's Enabled toggle.*
