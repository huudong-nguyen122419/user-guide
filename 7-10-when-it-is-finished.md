# 7.10 · When it is finished

> 7 · Manage a Marketing Email

> **Closing it is an Admin action, you watch it happen**
>
> You do not mark a ME finished. **Admin/Ops does**, the same people who ran it in [7.5 ↗](7-5-force-send-run-admin.md). Your side of the ending is **monitoring**: keep reading the numbers ([7.6b ↗](7-6b-monitor-the-me.md)) until the queue has drained and the replies have stopped arriving, answer what is still open, and tell Admin it is done from your end. The checklist below is still yours: it is what you work through **before** handing it back, not something you tick after somebody flips a status.

## 7.10.1 · The system decides “Finished”. You decide “done”

They are not the same moment, and the gap between them is where the work actually is.

|  | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Finished** (the status) | every queued email has left. Progress shows a full bar and a green ✓, the queue has nothing in **In Queue**. **This is about mail, not about outcomes.** |
| **Done** (your call) | every reply has been read, answered and labelled; every bounce has been chased another way; every hot lead has been moved on. **Nothing in the product marks this: you do.** |

## 7.10.2 · The closing checklist

Run it before you stop looking at the marketing email:

| Check | Where | What you want to see |
|---|---|---|
| Nothing still queued | Email Queue → **In Queue** | **0**. Anything left will still go out. |
| Delivered matches what you sent | the row's **Delivered** vs the people you reviewed | the same number. A gap means someone was skipped: find out who. |
| Bounce / Dropped cleared | the row's **Bounce/Dropped Rate** | either **0**, or every one of them chased another way ([7.8 ↗](7-8-replies-bounce-drop.md)). |
| No unlabelled replies | Inbox → category rail → **No Categories** | empty. |
| Opt-outs actually recorded | the same rail | anyone who asked to stop carries **Opted Out** or **Do Not Contact**, not a softer label. |
| Hot replies moved on | Lifecycle Stage on the contact | Interested / Call Scheduled set, and the meeting booked ([Flow 4b ↗](4b-schedule-a-call.md)). |

## 7.10.3 · Finished does not mean frozen

Replies keep arriving after the last email lands, often for weeks. The Inbox stays live, so **keep checking it after the status has moved on**. A blast that reads Finished on Monday can still produce a meeting on Friday.

## 7.10.4 · Read the rates once at the end, and read them honestly

A blank rate means nothing was sent. **0%** means it went out and nobody opened or replied. That is a content result, not a delivery one, and it is the number worth raising before the next send. Reply Rate is the one that matters; Open Rate mostly tells you about the subject line.
