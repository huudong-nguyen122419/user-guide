# B5.1 · Who can see them

> B5 · Talent · find a resource

1. **B5.1.1** — **Resources are behind the signup fee.** A talent who has not paid gets nothing — the request is refused at the server, not filtered in the page. Talents who have paid see the full published set. The refusal does not look like a refusalAn unpaid talent is shown **“No resources found — Try adjusting your search or filter”**. That reads as an empty search. They will change the filters, find nothing, and conclude the library is empty rather than locked. If a talent reports “there are no resources”, check whether they have paid before you go looking for a bug.

2. **B5.1.2** — **Only Published reaches them.** The talent side cannot ask for Draft or Archived at all — the server decides, so there is no filter a talent could set that would expose unfinished material.
