# 6.5.3 · Skip a step

> 6 · Manage a campaign → 6.5 · Preview & review

**Not every contact should get every step. Skip the ones that should not.** Each step row carries a **Skip** button, and skipping is per step **and** per contact: **that one person will not receive that one email**, while everybody else still does. **What that looks like when the campaign runs.** Say the sequence has two steps, **A** and **B**: contact X step A (skipped) step B contact Y step A step B when step A goes out → only Y receives it when step B goes out → X and Y both receive it X is still in the campaign and still on the People tab. They are not removed, not stopped, and not unsubscribed. They simply sit out that one email and rejoin at the next. **The buttons and what each one reaches**, including **Skip all main step**, how to undo it with **Rollback Skip**, and the order that costs you the bulk button: [6.x.13 · Skip ↗](6-x-13-skip-a-step-not-sent.md).
