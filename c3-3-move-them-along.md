# C3.3 · Move them along

> C3 · Work the applications

The **Actions** column carries the stage changes, and they read as plain sentences rather than a dropdown: **Switch to Applied**, **Move back to Shortlisted**. What is offered depends on where the application already is, so you cannot skip a stage by accident.

**View Application** opens what they actually submitted, including their answers to the screening questions. The **Connected** chip tells you a conversation with this person already exists.

> **Moving the stage is not telling the person**
>
> The stage is a record, not a message. **Rejected** does not write to anybody, and neither does **Presented**. Somebody who applied three weeks ago and has heard nothing is the most common complaint in this flow, and the fix is a message, not a stage change ([Flow 3 ↗](3-send-a-1-1-email.md)).
